This policy describes how the Atlassian Marketplace app "Create Links – Prefilled Create Issue Links for Jira" (the "App") processes data. The App is provided by **Bc. Tomáš Buchta, sole trader, Company ID (IČO) 21540951, registered office 30. dubna 2307/12, 702 00 Ostrava, Czech Republic** ("we"). Contact: **[support@tsutsei-support.atlassian.net](mailto:support@tsutsei-support.atlassian.net)**.

## 1. What the App stores
Only **saved links** created by space or Jira admins: the link's name, the space (project) and work type it opens, and the default field values chosen for it. The App stores **no personal data**: no account IDs, names, e-mail addresses, and nothing about who opened or used a link. The assignee of a saved link can only be "the person who opens the link", resolved when the link is opened.

Links that are not saved are not stored at all – their values are part of the link itself.

## 2. What the App reads
To build links, the App shows the spaces, work types and create-screen fields of the signed-in user, read from Jira **with that user's permissions**. To check who may save or delete a shared link it reads the user's permissions. After a work item is created, it looks up the new work item's key to show a link to it. None of this is stored.

## 3. Where the data is stored
All data is stored in Atlassian's infrastructure (Forge Key-Value Store) and inherits Atlassian's data residency. The App does not send data to any server outside Atlassian, and we (the vendor) have no access to it.

## 4. Purpose and legal basis
Saved links are processed solely to provide the App's function on behalf of the Jira site, which acts as the controller; we act as processor (Art. 28 GDPR) for any personal data a customer chooses to type into link values (for example a name in a summary).

## 5. Retention
Saved links are kept until they are deleted in the App. When the App is uninstalled, Atlassian deletes the App's data according to its Forge data retention policy.

## 6. Data Processing Addendum
The [Data Processing Addendum]({{ '/dpa/' | relative_url }}) (Art. 28 GDPR) applies to all customers.

## 7. Sharing
We do not sell or share data. Sub-processor: Atlassian (hosting of the Forge platform).

## 8. Your rights
Admins can delete saved links and uninstall the App at any time. For requests concerning personal data, contact your Jira site admin or us at [support@tsutsei-support.atlassian.net](mailto:support@tsutsei-support.atlassian.net).
